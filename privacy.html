Privacy policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026  ·  Developer: Saddam Jamali  ·  Contact: umairahmedjamali@gmail.com

This policy explains what the Habar app (package com.nairobi.habar) collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: Habar has no ads, no analytics and no location tracking, and it does not sell your data.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Anonymous user ID (a random ID created by Firebase Authentication)Automatically, the first time you open the appTo save your progress in the cloud
Email address and passwordOnly if you choose to create an account. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it in readable form.To sign you in and let you restore your progress on a new phone
Learning progress: XP, day streak, lessons completed, best scores and attempts, and your practice scheduleAs you use the appTo run the app and sync your progress
Technical data processed by Firebase: IP address (for security and abuse prevention), a Firebase installation ID, and the app/platform versionAutomatically when the app talks to FirebaseSecurity, and keeping the service working

Kept on your device only: your hearts, your sound on/off setting, and a local copy of your progress.

What we do not collect

Habar does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise or approximate location, or advertising ID, and it shows no ads.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Account and progress data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, Europe region). Google processes it on our behalf as a service provider under Firebase's privacy terms. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and Firestore security rules allow each user to read and write only their own record.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete it. You can delete your account and all saved progress inside the app (Profile → Account → Delete account, or Delete my data if you have not created an account), or by following the steps on the delete your account page. Deleting removes your account, your saved progress on our servers, and the local copy on that device.

Children

Habar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

The About screen links to the developer's Facebook and LinkedIn pages and to this website. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Open content

Word lists in the app come from open sources, including Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0). This content is bundled inside the app and does not involve sending your data anywhere.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
