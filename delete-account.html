Delete your account and data

You can delete your Habar account and everything saved with it at any time.

Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Habar and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account (or Create an account if you never made one).
  3. Tap Delete account (or Delete my data). If you have an email account you will be asked for your password.
  4. Confirm. Your account and saved progress are deleted immediately.

Option 2 – by email

If you no longer have the app, email umairahmedjamali@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Habar account deletion. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address, or your anonymous ID)
  • Your saved learning progress: XP, streak, completed lessons, scores and practice schedule
  • The local copy of your progress on the device you used

Some technical logs kept by Google for security may persist for a limited time under Google's own retention policies.

See also our privacy policy.
